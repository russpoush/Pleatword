Pleatword#1

Fold the paper until a 5-letter word shows up in a row. 0 folds · par 2
Tap a crease handle (the little bars) to choose where to fold.

This is a sheet of origami paper with a letter on each square. The front is white. The back is colored, and its letter shows in the little corner chip.

Tap a crease handle, then pick which side to fold over. A folded flap flips, so you see its back letters, in mirror order.

Goal: make a real 5-letter word appear, left to right, in any row. Par is the fewest folds that can do it.
